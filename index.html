Our Philosophy

Architectural Simplicity

At XIV COLLECTIONS, we design garments that resist the cycle of fast trends. We partner exclusively with certified organic textile mills in Portugal and Italy to produce pieces built for everyday longevity.

"Simplicity is not the absence of clutter, but the deliberate focus on proportion, texture, and lasting form."

The XIV Promise

  • Zero Waste: Batch production avoiding oversupply.
  • Organic Mills: 100% traceably sourced cotton and silk.
  • Complimentary Care: Lifetime button and seam repair on all trousers.
